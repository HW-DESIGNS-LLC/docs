Crescent City — V2.0

An ESP32‑S3 Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth LE development board with a built-in 0.96″ OLED, USB‑C, and protected single-cell battery charging. Breadboard-friendly, fully documented, and running out of the box.

Board V2.0 · EE2026003B Status Preliminary Updated October 6, 2026
Print / save as PDF Board docs DVT report

Highlights

What you get

  • ESP32‑S3, dual-core 240 MHz, 8 MB flash, Wi‑Fi 4 and Bluetooth 5 LE
  • 0.96″ 128×64 OLED built in — no wiring, working out of the box
  • Complete battery power: USB‑C charging, cell protection, automatic USB/battery switching and battery monitoring
  • Qwiic / STEMMA QT connector plus 12 GPIO on breadboard-friendly headers
  • Ships with an open-source Weather Clock; Arduino and ESPHome / Home Assistant ready
  • Published schematic, 3D model and a 122-test validation report

Specifications

Specifications

measured V1.0 bench measurement on the V1.0 prototypes (see the DVT report). V2.0 target design value, confirmed during V2.0 testing (Nov–Dec 2026). All other values are from the design.

Processor & wireless

ModuleESP32‑S3‑WROOM‑1‑N8 with on-module PCB antenna
ProcessorDual-core Xtensa LX7, up to 240 MHz
Memory512 KB SRAM · 8 MB flash · no PSRAM
Wi‑Fi802.11 b/g/n (Wi‑Fi 4), 2.4 GHz
BluetoothBluetooth 5 (LE)

Display

Type0.96″ white monochrome OLED, 128 × 64 pixels
ControllerSSD1306, 4-wire SPI on dedicated pins (not shared with the headers)
Display pinsCS GPIO10 · DC GPIO9 · SCLK GPIO12 · MOSI GPIO11 · RESET GPIO14

USB & programming

ConnectorUSB‑C, USB 2.0 full speed (native ESP32‑S3 USB)
Used forPower, charging, programming, serial console and debugging. No driver needed on Windows 10/11, macOS or Linux.
CompatibilityWorks with USB‑C chargers and C‑to‑C cables; ESD protection on the data lines
ButtonsBOOT (GPIO0, also usable as a user button) and EN (reset)

Power & battery

Power input5 V from USB‑C, or the 5V_IN header pin (input only, with a 0.5 A resettable fuse and a blocking diode)
BatterySingle-cell 3.7 V Li‑ion / LiPo, JST‑PH 2.0 mm connector, polarity marked. 600 mAh or larger recommended.
Charging≈300 mA constant current to 4.20 V, then tapers and stops at ≈7.5% of the charge current V2.0 target
V1.0: 201 mA, 4.19 V, stopped at 7.6% measured V1.0
Charge timeTo be measured on V2.0
V1.0 at 200 mA: about 5 h per 1000 mAh (estimate for a directly connected cell)
Battery protectionOver-charge, over-discharge (cuts off at ≈2.4 V), over-current / short circuit, reverse battery polarity
Power switchingAutomatic: runs from USB when plugged in (battery isolated), switches to the battery when USB is removed
3.3 V regulatorLow-dropout regulator, 600 mA
MonitoringBattery voltage on GPIO4 and USB voltage on GPIO2, each through a ½ divider (ADC1)
Low-battery shutdownThe Weather Clock and ESPHome firmware shut down cleanly before the 3.3 V rail sags, and wake when USB is plugged in V2.0 target

Power consumption (typical, 3.8 V battery)

Weather Clock, Wi‑Fi on≈70 mA — about 14 hours per 1000 mAh measured V1.0
Wi‑Fi transmit peaksUp to ≈400 mA for microseconds measured V1.0
Deep sleep≈0.3 mA V2.0 target
V1.0: 0.88 mA measured V1.0; V2.0 lowers the power-LED current
After protection cutoff≈1 µA measured V1.0

I/O & expansion

Header pins2 × 12, 0.1″ (2.54 mm) pitch, rows 0.9″ (22.86 mm) apart — fits a standard breadboard
GPIO on headers12: GPIO 1, 5, 6, 7, 13, 15, 16, 21, 35, 36, 37, 39
Analog inputsGPIO 1, 5, 6, 7 (ADC1, usable with Wi‑Fi on); GPIO 13, 15, 16 (ADC2)
SerialUART0 on the header (pins marked TX and RX), 3.3 V logic
Qwiic / STEMMA QTJST‑SH 4-pin, I²C on GPIO8 (SDA) and GPIO18 (SCL), 3.3 V, 4.7 kΩ pull-ups
Power pins3V3 ×3 · GND ×6 · 5V_IN ×1 (input only)
Logic level3.3 V. GPIO are not 5 V tolerant.

LEDs

RGB status LEDAddressable SK6812 on GPIO48
CHGYellow, on while charging
SYS ONGreen, on when the 3.3 V rail is up

Mechanical

Board size70.1 × 25.4 mm (2.76″ × 1.00″); ≈76.4 mm including the antenna overhang
Height13 mm overall, with the display fitted
Weight14 g
Mounting holesNone — mount by the header pins or in a breadboard
PCB4-layer
3D modelSTEP file for enclosure design

Software

Ships withWeather Clock: local time and weather over Wi‑Fi, with Wi‑Fi setup from your phone. Open source (MIT license).
Arduino“esp32 by Espressif Systems” board package, board “ESP32S3 Dev Module”
ESPHome / Home AssistantReady-made configuration: clock and status pages, battery level, Qwiic sensor, RGB LED, updates over Wi‑Fi
OtherESP‑IDF, MicroPython and CircuitPython should work with their generic ESP32‑S3 builds (not yet tested by us)

Environment & compliance

Operating temperature−40 to +85 °C (ESP32‑S3 module rating). With a battery fitted, stay within the battery’s own limits.
Charging temperature0 to 45 °C (battery limit; the charger has no temperature sensor)
CertificationFCC / ISED testing is planned before shipping. The ESP32‑S3 module itself is pre-certified.
